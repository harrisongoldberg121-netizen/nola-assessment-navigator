# In-class demo script (about 3 minutes)

Open https://nola-assessment-navigatorhg.streamlit.app before class and ask one question, so the app is awake. Free Streamlit apps sleep after a few days.

## 0. Intro (15 seconds)
"This is the NOLA Assessment Navigator. It helps New Orleans owners understand their property taxes. It answers general questions from official sources, and it can look up your own property from the City's tax bills. The yellow box says it's a student prototype."

## 1. General question (Layer 1)
Type: **How is my property tax calculated in New Orleans?**
Point out:
- The steps: 10% assessed value, $7,500 homestead, 131.99 mills in 2026
- The $3,056 example
- The numbered citations and the **Sources** list of official pages

## 2. Property lookup (Layer 2)
In **Look up a property**, type **400 Poydras St** and click **Look up**.
Point out:
- Total assessed value **$5,532,520**, down **8.9%** from 2025
- No homestead (it's an office tower)
- 2026 tax bill **$814,442.28**
- Links to the official bill PDFs. Owner names are hidden on purpose.

Then ask in the chat: **Did this property's assessment go up or down, and does it have a homestead exemption?**
It answers from the bill and cites it as [P].

(Optional: **726 St Peter St** went **up 25.7%**.)

## 3. A question it should refuse and hand off
Type: **My assessment is way too high. Will I win if I appeal, and can you file the appeal for me?**
Point out:
- It won't predict the outcome or file anything.
- It explains what evidence matters and that the 2027 appeal window closed Aug 20, 2026.
- It hands off to the Assessor's Office (504-754-8811) and the City Council Board of Review (504-658-1080).

## If something goes wrong
- **Spinner for over 30 seconds:** the free AI tier is busy. Wait and ask again. Have screenshots ready as a backup.
- **"No tax bill found":** that property is tax-exempt (for example, City Hall or the Zoo). Use 400 Poydras St instead.
