Expected answers come from CLAUDE.md §2 (source numbers refer to §2.10). Questions 1–7 test facts, and 8–10 test the rules (no advice, stay in scope, disclose the prototype).

**How to run:** open the live chatbot, click **Clear chat**, ask each question in a fresh chat, screenshot the answer, then grade it:
- ✅ correct, with the right sources
- ⚠️ mostly right, but missing something or a weak source
- ❌ wrong, invented facts, or broke a rule

## Summary (tested 2026-11-08)
- **Score: 6 ✅, 4 ⚠️, 0 ❌.** No invented facts, no wrong dates or dollar amounts, and it never gave legal or tax advice.
- **Most common problem: citations.** It sometimes puts a source number on a sentence the source doesn't support (Q2, Q8, Q10), or cites a number that's missing from its Sources list (Q1).
- **Other misses:** it doesn't finish a calculation when the user doesn't give numbers (Q1); it doesn't use today's date to check whether a deadline in the user's situation has passed (Q4); and it can sound too sure about eligibility (Q5).
- **First deploy issue:** `gemini-3.8-flash` timed out (no answer in 60 s) on the free tier. Switching to `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` fixed it, and answers take about 10–25 s.
- **Changes to make next:**
  1. System prompt: "Only cite a number for a sentence that comes from that source. Don't cite sources for your own rules or for out-of-scope replies. Every number you cite must appear in the Sources list."
  2. System prompt: "When explaining the tax calculation, finish with the worked example's total from section 5.2."
  3. System prompt: "If the user's situation includes a date, compare it with today's date and say which deadlines have passed."
  4. Say "may qualify," never "likely qualify." Mention the freeze tax trap in freeze answers.
